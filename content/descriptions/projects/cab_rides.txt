# Cab Rides Pricing Dynamics & Surge Multiplier Analytics

A comprehensive data analytics and visualization study examining multi-variable cab ride pricing dynamics, surge multiplier impacts, temporal demand patterns, and vehicle class revenue contributions across 600,000+ ride transactions.

## Analytical Inquiries & Key Findings
- **Temporal & Seasonal Revenue Concentration**: December 2018 accounted for 20% of annual revenue, with peak evening hours (18:00–20:00) generating over 30% of daily ride income.
- **Surge Multiplier Dynamics**: 70% of rides operated at base fare (1.0x surge), while high-tier surges (≥2.0x) were concentrated in premium services (Lux Black, Uber Black), exhibiting a 0.50 correlation with final gross fare.
- **Trip Distance & Revenue Distribution**: Short-to-medium trips (3–5 miles) dominated aggregate revenue generation (60% of total revenue).
- **Service Tier Economics**: Premium services like Lux Black XL and Black SUV led total gross revenue generation, while Shared rides contributed the least (2.8%).
