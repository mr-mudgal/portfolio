# Indian Cuisine Culinary Analytics & Flavor Profile Mining

An extensive exploratory data analysis and culinary intelligence study mining dietary preferences, regional dish distributions, flavor spectrums, preparation timelines, and ingredient dependencies across authentic Indian regional cuisines.

## Key Analytical Findings & Insights
- **Dietary Composition**: Vegetarian preparations represent 88.6% of the national dataset, reaching 100% in Western states such as Gujarat.
- **Flavor Profile Taxonomy**: Spicy preparations dominate 52.2% of recorded dishes, with sweet courses comprising 34.5% (concentrated heavily in West and East India).
- **Culinary Time Economics**: 60% of all recipes require between 30 and 120 minutes of preparation, with mean cook time (34 min) outpacing prep time (30 min).
- **Ingredient Centrality**: Urad Dal, Ghee, Sugar, and Ginger represent dominant culinary anchors across vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.

## Tools & Technical Architecture
- **Language & Analytics**: Python 3.x, Pandas (Data Cleaning & Categorical Binning), Matplotlib, Seaborn, WordCloud

## Steps to Run Locally
1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/mr-mudgal/indian-food-analysis.git`
2. Install analytical dependencies: `pip install pandas matplotlib seaborn wordcloud`
