# Cinematic AI Podcast & Cultural Narrative Studio — Qwen-2.5 + Kokoro TTS

An automated, end-to-end generative storytelling and cinematic podcast production engine that converts raw historical and mythological markdown texts into high-retention audio scripts using `Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, synthesizes expressive neural speech via a blended `Kokoro ONNX` model (70% Indian Female + 30% Warm Emotional Female), and masters the final multi-track mix with automated BGM ducking (-19dB), 700ms dramatic pacing, and smooth outro fades.

**🎙️ Official Podcast Channel**: [Listen on Spotify & Platforms (The Narrative Network)](https://linktr.ee/thenarrativenetwork) · `linktr.ee/thenarrativenetwork`

---

## 🎯 System Architecture & 4-Stage Production Lifecycle

The pipeline automates the entire studio workflow from raw document extraction to studio-grade broadcast audio mastering:

```
┌───────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Phase 1: Ingest & Clean  │ ────> │  Phase 2: Narrative Script   │
│  Markdown / 2500-char Chunk│       │  Qwen2.5-7B (fp16 / Dual T4) │
└───────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────┘
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
┌───────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Phase 4: Pydub Mastering │ <──── │  Phase 3: Neural Voice Synthesis │
│  BGM Ducking (-19dB) + WAV│       │  Kokoro ONNX (70/30 Voice Blend)│
└───────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 🧹 Phase 1: Markdown Cleaning & Semantic Chunking
- **Regex Purification**: Strips code blocks, inline markdown formatting, images, URLs, asterisks, and blockquotes while preserving narrative paragraph structure.
- **Dynamic 2500-Character Windows**: Partitions large texts into coherent chunks along paragraph boundaries (`\n\n`) to maintain conversational context without cutting sentences mid-thought.

---

## 🎬 Phase 2: Qwen-2.5 High-Retention Script Engine
- **Model Stack**: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` initialized in `torch.float16` with `device_map="auto"`.
- **Psychological Retention Hooks**: Rewrites raw excerpts into immersive, cinematic audio scripts spoken from the perspective of an authentic female Indian narrator.
- **Storytelling Directives**:
  1. Instant hook in the opening seconds.
  2. Evocative sensory descriptions (air temperature, echoing ancient stones, cosmic vibrations).
  3. Strategic "open loops" and mini-cliffhangers at paragraph boundaries to drive listener retention.
  4. Pure spoken words with all bracketed meta-tags (`[SFX]`, `(cues)`) stripped.
- **VRAM Lifecycle & Zero-Leak Flush**: Programmatically invokes `del model, tokenizer, inputs, outputs`, `gc.collect()`, and `torch.cuda.empty_cache()` to completely clear VRAM before TTS synthesis.

---

## 🎙️ Phase 3: Kokoro Neural TTS & Custom Voice Blending
- **ONNX Inference Engine**: Deploys `Kokoro-v1.0.onnx` with `voices-v1.0.bin` for ultra-fast, CPU/GPU-accelerated speech synthesis.
- **Custom Indian Female Voice Blend**: Formulates a weighted latent style vector:
  $$\vec{V}_{\text{voice}} = 0.70 \cdot \vec{V}_{\text{hf\_alpha}} + 0.30 \cdot \vec{V}_{\text{af\_heart}}$$
  Blending authentic Indian pronunciation (`hf_alpha`) with warm emotional vocal resonance (`af_heart`).
- **Cinematic Cadence & Dramatic Timing**: Synthesizes speech at `speed=0.82` with automated `+700ms` silence intervals (`AudioSegment.silent(duration=700)`) between paragraphs for dramatic narrative weight.

---

## 🎛️ Phase 4: Audio Engineering, Dynamic BGM Ducking & Master Export
- **Dynamic Soundscape Layering**: Scans a curated background music directory, selects an atmospheric Indian ambient track, and auto-loops it to match the exact narration length.
- **Intelligent Audio Ducking**: Attenuates background music by **-19.0 dB** (`bgm_track - 19`) under the voice track to ensure speech intelligibility.
- **Cinematic Fade Tail**: Appends a 4,000ms smooth audio fade-out tail (`fade_out(4000)`) for a broadcast-ready conclusion.
- **Deliverables**: Exports high-fidelity `Final_Premium_Podcast.wav` master audio alongside `Final_Script.txt` for YouTube and podcast descriptions.
